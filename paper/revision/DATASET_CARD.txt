Annotated Egocentric-10K: derived resource card
Revision date: 19 October 2026

Purpose and ownership
This repository supports research on inferring, assessing, and interpreting
operational event logs from video. It contains derived model outputs from
Build AI's Egocentric-10K, not the source video collection. The manuscript
lists Alessandro Berti and Wil M. P. van der Aalst as authors.

Composition
The processed subset covers factory identifiers 001 to 006, 59 worker
identifiers, and 322 recording identifiers. It includes 322 transcripts,
59 worker summaries, six factory reports, 322 per-video CSVs containing
10,698 events, 59 merged worker CSVs, and six merged factory CSVs. Each
merged layer repeats the same events. There are no empty per-video logs.
The original parser recognizes 13,304 transcript entries; recognizing
MM:SS and mixed formats gives 13,462 entries without time rescaling.
Factory reports embed separate activity and process lists. There are 113
factory-specific process entries, 108 distinct strings, and 112 used
entries. The one unused process entry has no original category assignment.

Collection and selection
The provider describes head-mounted recordings in real factories. Factory,
worker, and recording identifiers come from the source organization. Domain
and process descriptions are model interpretations, not verified business
metadata. Selection rationale, sampling coverage, original source revision,
and extraction dates are unavailable in the derived archive. Do not treat
the subset as a random or representative sample.

Source reference
https://huggingface.co/datasets/builddotai/Egocentric-10K
The card viewed on 19 October 2026 states a median clip length of 180 seconds,
Apache 2.0 licensing, and per-video duration_sec metadata in TAR archives.
The current repository revision is recorded in source_metadata.json. It is
not the unknown revision used for extraction. Metadata for these particular
recordings is absent locally. Public raw-file retrieval returned HTTP 401;
no restricted source archive was downloaded. The current card's original
consent procedure is not documented. No collection consent or secondary
ethics approval is asserted by this resource card.

Processing and fields
S1 narrates video; S2 combines a worker's transcripts; S3 produces a factory
report and vocabularies; S4 extracts permitted activity/process labels and
estimated interval boundaries; S5 merges CSVs. Scripts use no task training
or manual input annotations. Assessment includes author judgments and a
manual category map. Model strings are script defaults, not per-request
historical verification. Full prompts are in the repository and appendix.

Per-video fields: start_timestamp, end_timestamp, activity, process,
factory, worker. Video identity is in the path. Merged fields additionally
include source_file, case_id, start_seconds_from_video_start,
end_seconds_from_video_start, duration_seconds. Dates are synthetic relative
to 2000-01-01, not calendar dates. Merging orders filenames and advances the
offset by the maximum extracted endpoint. Worker clocks are independent.

Time quantities and exclusions
Verified source footage duration: unavailable.
Original parsed endpoint spans: 786,412 seconds, not footage duration.
Format-aware literal endpoint spans: 786,418 seconds, still ambiguous.
All-log event interval unions: 414,886 seconds.
All-log summed event durations: 421,283 seconds.
Retained time-analysis summed event durations: 349,659 seconds in 320 logs
and 10,647 rows. The two exclusions have event endpoints over 3,600 seconds.
Seven transcripts have literal endpoints over one hour; four additional
files contain MM:SS, including three that change format. No automatic repair
is applied. Thirty-five adjacent event pairs overlap. The manifests expose
all formats, endpoints, transitions, and inclusion decisions.

Assessment
All rows have required values and permitted labels. The existing author
audit includes 36 fully supported events, six partially supported, and zero
contradicted, using transcripts as reference. Its entry check has 34 COV,
four EXC, and four MISS judgments. Sampling seed 42 and seven items per
factory are asserted in the archive; the sampling implementation is absent.
Video accuracy, inter-rater agreement, and intervention effects are unmeasured.

Intended uses
Reproduce frozen-artifact calculations; inspect evidence paths; study event
abstraction, case selection, category mapping, and uncertainty; formulate
process-design hypotheses requiring independent follow-up.

Unsuitable uses
Employee appraisal, automated rankings, disciplinary or pay decisions,
throughput estimates from event counts, causal diagnoses from category
shares, and capacity or cost-saving estimates without additional evidence.
A process-based zero does not establish absence of an activity. Defect
patching duration does not establish defect rates or financial losses.

Privacy, access, and distribution
Worker identifiers are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. The derived
repository is public; source footage remains subject to provider access
terms. This revision did not submit or redistribute footage. Workplace
analyses should limit access to their stated purpose, aggregate suitable
task groups, and retain provenance and coverage limitations. The provider's
license is not a statement that every downstream workplace use is appropriate.
The repository includes an Apache 2.0 LICENSE file; this revision does not
change it.

Maintenance and reproducibility
paper/reproduce.py recalculates descriptive results with Python's standard
library and verifies all 65 merged files. artifact_manifest.csv contains
hashes of frozen outputs. Model regeneration is separate and may not exactly
reproduce historical outputs. See REVISION_NOTES.txt for unresolved history,
requirements_completion.csv for the revision map, and claim_evidence.csv for
supporting artifacts and calculation definitions.
