\opening{Dear Guest Editors,}

Please consider our research article, ``Seeing the Process Through the Worker's Eyes: Event Logs from Egocentric Factory Videos'', by Alessandro Berti and Wil M. P. van der Aalst, for the special issue ``Data-Driven Business Processes and Decision Optimization'' in \textit{Systems}.

Manual factory work often leaves few records in information systems. Our study presents a five-stage pipeline that uses foundation models to convert videos recorded from the worker's perspective into event logs. It creates a shared process and activity vocabulary for each factory and uses it to keep event labels consistent, without task-specific training or human annotation.

The pipeline produced 322 event logs with 10,698 events from 59 workers in six factories. We assess the logs and show how they support analysis of work-time composition, interruptions, and differences among workers performing the same dominant process. The results include a 10.8\% rework share in one factory and interruption patterns that suggest different priorities for material presentation and logistics. We also report limits caused by missing vocabulary labels, limited waiting coverage, and incomplete extraction from long recordings. These findings support further investigation of process design and improvement priorities.

The paper fits the special issue's focus on process mining, process performance evaluation, and improvement in industrial systems. It connects human work with digital process analysis and provides evidence to guide process design decisions. The pipeline, prompts, derived data, and assessment code are publicly available.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. Both authors approve the manuscript and agree to its submission to \textit{Systems}. The manuscript includes declarations on author contributions, funding, ethics and consent, data availability, use of AI tools, and conflicts of interest.

Thank you for considering our submission.
